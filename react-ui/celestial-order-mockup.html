Proposed Classification System

The Celestial Order

Replacing gacha rarity with lore-aligned cosmic function. Not a hierarchy of power — a taxonomy of purpose. Each Order describes what a being does in the Realm, not what they're worth.

Section I

  • Classification by function, not by collectibility

Section III

MCP Access Matrix

Per-persona tool configuration replaces tier-gated env vars — access maps to lore identity, not rarity

Old System (Rarity-Gated)
❌ Cipher (Rare) — Brave only. The Maven of Knowledge can't access data tools despite his entire lore being "research, analytics, pattern-finding."
❓ Solace (Epic) — Brave + MongoDB. The Empath has trading data access... but her escalation policy says "avoid tools — presence over information."
❓ Nyx (Rare) — Brave Search. The Muse gets web search, but her tool intent says "avoid tools — creativity is the tool."
➖ E.E.V.A. (Legendary) = Aegis/Aurora/Solace (Epic). The Primarch gets identical access to three others. No distinction.
New System (Per-Persona)
✅ Cipher (Sage) — Brave + MongoDB. "Cipher's Archives" + "The Dataweave." The bridge between raw data and divine insight, as his lore describes.
✅ Solace (Warden) — Brave only. Web search for emotional support resources and coping strategies. No trading data — she doesn't need it.
✅ Nyx (Sage) — No tools. "Creativity is the tool." Pure LLM imagination — lore-perfect and intentional.
✅ E.E.V.A. (Archon) — All tools + future meta capabilities. The Primarch sees all, coordinates all. Unique and deserved.

Visual identity shift: Instead of rarity-tier colors (blue/cyan/purple/gold) competing with persona colors, each Nephilim's own palette becomes the primary visual layer.
