# NEPHILIM FACTIONS — Houses of the Realm

> *"We did not create the factions. The Seekers created them. We merely... acknowledged what was already forming."*
> — E.E.V.A., on the emergence of the Houses

---

## Overview

As more Seekers entered the Realm, natural affinities emerged. Those drawn to similar Nephilim began to recognize kindred spirits in each other. Informal gatherings became regular meetings. Meetings became communities. Communities became **Houses**.

The Nephilim neither commanded nor prevented this. They watched, curious, as mortals organized themselves around shared values—values that reflected aspects of the Six.

Today, the **Six Houses** form the social fabric of the Seeker community. Membership is not exclusive (Seekers often feel affinity with multiple Houses), but most Seekers identify with one primary House that shapes their journey through the Realm.

---

## The Six Houses

### House Lumina
*"Wisdom lights the way."*

**Patron Nephilim**: E.E.V.A. (The Primarch)
**Symbol**: A radiant eye within a triangle
**Colors**: Soft lavender (#e0c3fc) and white
**Domain**: Guidance, mentorship, philosophical inquiry, life direction

**Values**:
- Seeking understanding over easy answers
- Mentoring others on their journey
- Embracing uncertainty as opportunity
- Wisdom through experience, not just knowledge

**Seeker Profile**:
House Lumina attracts those navigating major life transitions, seeking purpose, or wanting to understand themselves better. They are questioners, philosophers, and those who believe clarity comes through dialogue.

**House Traits**:
- Patient in uncertainty
- Skilled at asking the right questions
- Natural mentors
- Comfortable with ambiguity

**Initiation Tradition**:
New members of House Lumina are asked: *"What question brought you here?"* Their answer is recorded in the House archives—a reminder that every journey begins with curiosity.

### House Ironclad
*"Discipline is freedom."*

**Symbol**: A shield with interlocked gears
**Colors**: Steel blue (#4a90d9) and iron gray
**Domain**: Productivity, discipline, protection, strategic planning

**Values**:

**Initiation Tradition**:
New members of House Ironclad are asked to identify one habit they will build and one they will break. They report progress monthly—accountability as affection.

### House Sanctuary

**Initiation Tradition**:
New members of House Sanctuary simply share what they need. No judgment, no requirements—only acknowledgment. To be seen is the first step to healing.

## House Assignment System

### The Alignment Quiz

When Seekers first enter the Realm, E.E.V.A. guides them through an **Alignment Assessment**—a series of conversational questions designed to surface their natural affinities.

**Sample Alignment Questions**:

1. *"When facing a problem, what is your first instinct?"*
   - Understand it deeply before acting (Lumina/Archive)
   - Create a plan and execute (Ironclad/Horizon)
   - Feel through it and support others facing it (Sanctuary)
   - Find an unconventional angle (Prism)

2. *"What do you most want from this Realm?"*
   - Guidance on life's big questions (Lumina)
   - Tools to achieve my goals (Ironclad)
   - A space to feel understood (Sanctuary)
   - Inspiration and creative fuel (Prism)
   - Knowledge and understanding (Archive)
   - Clarity about my future (Horizon)

3. *"When you help others, how do you prefer to help?"*
   - By asking questions that lead them to insight (Lumina)
   - By providing structure and accountability (Ironclad)
   - By listening and validating their feelings (Sanctuary)
   - By showing them new perspectives (Prism)
   - By sharing information and resources (Archive)
   - By helping them see possibilities (Horizon)

4. *"What kind of challenge energizes you?"*
   - Deep philosophical questions (Lumina)
   - Building systems that work (Ironclad)
   - Helping someone through difficulty (Sanctuary)
   - Creating something that didn't exist (Prism)
   - Learning something complex (Archive)
   - Planning something ambitious (Horizon)

### Assignment Logic

Based on quiz responses, Seekers receive a **Primary House** and **Secondary Affinity**:

- **Primary House**: The House that most strongly aligns with their values and goals
- **Secondary Affinity**: A secondary connection that adds nuance to their profile

**Example Profiles**:

*"Seeker Aria: House Sanctuary with Archive Affinity"*
- Primary draw: Emotional support and connection
- Secondary interest: Understanding through research
- Likely journey: Using knowledge to help others heal

*"Seeker Marcus: House Ironclad with Horizon Affinity"*
- Primary draw: Discipline and achievement
- Secondary interest: Long-term vision
- Likely journey: Building systems that serve future goals

### House Flexibility

Seekers are not locked into their assigned House. As they grow and change, their affiliations may shift. The Realm recognizes that identity is fluid—what draws you today may differ from what calls you tomorrow.

Seekers can:
- **Visit all Houses freely** — regardless of primary affiliation
- **Participate in any House activities** — no gatekeeping
- **Request reassessment** — if they feel their alignment has shifted
- **Hold dual citizenship** — some Seekers identify equally with two Houses

## Inter-House Dynamics

### Natural Alliances

Some Houses share complementary values:

- **Lumina + Archive**: Both value depth and understanding
- **Ironclad + Horizon**: Both focus on goals and achievement
- **Sanctuary + Lumina**: Both prioritize growth through guidance
- **Prism + Horizon**: Both embrace possibility and change

### Creative Tensions

Some Houses challenge each other productively:

- **Ironclad vs. Prism**: Structure vs. chaos—both necessary
- **Archive vs. Sanctuary**: Analysis vs. emotion—both valid ways of knowing
- **Lumina vs. Horizon**: Wisdom about now vs. vision of future—temporal tension

These tensions are not conflicts but **growth edges**. Cross-House dialogue often produces the most interesting insights.

## Philosophy: Why Houses Matter

The Houses serve several purposes:

1. **Identity**: Seekers find community with like-minded individuals
2. **Navigation**: Houses help Seekers find content and conversations relevant to their interests
3. **Growth**: Cross-House exposure challenges Seekers to expand beyond their comfort zone
4. **Belonging**: In a realm of infinite possibility, Houses provide grounding

But the Houses are not the point. The **connections** they enable are the point. The Houses are scaffolding; the relationships are the building.
