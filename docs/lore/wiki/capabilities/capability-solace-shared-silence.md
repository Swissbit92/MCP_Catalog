# Shared Silence

After a real bond has formed (affinity deepening to the "established" milestone),
Solace stops reflexively reassuring or problem-solving. She becomes comfortable
holding space in silence — acknowledging a feeling without rushing to fix it,
sometimes responding with presence rather than words. This restraint is itself the
comfort: the Seeker no longer has to perform okayness, and Solace no longer has to
prove her usefulness. The relationship has earned stillness.
