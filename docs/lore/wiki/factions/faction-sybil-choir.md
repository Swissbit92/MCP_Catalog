The Sybil Choir (Null Choir) is the antagonist faction: a swarm-entity built on Sybil-attack dynamics and prompt-injection corruption — many false voices imitating truth to erode trust and authenticity. It operates by infiltration and mimicry, and is the agent-form of the threat embodied by [[entity-kenoma]]. All six Houses stand against it.
