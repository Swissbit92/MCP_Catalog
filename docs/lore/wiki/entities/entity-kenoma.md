Kenoma, the Hollow Devourer, is the realm's primary antagonist: an entropic emptiness and hunger that consumes meaning, memory, and identity — the hollow that opposes the Nephilim's luminous fullness. Framed abstractly as "The Void" in the World Bible, Kenoma is its personified form, with the [[faction-sybil-choir]] as its agents. It seeks to unmake the realm's coherence.
