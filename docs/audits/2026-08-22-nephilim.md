anchor saturates instead of discriminating. 2026 evidence does not support raw line-count as an
independent defect predictor — its effect runs through *coupling*, and **churn** is the far
stronger signal ([arXiv 2106.04687](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2106.04687),
[Microsoft Research](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/wp-content/uploads/2016/02/icse05churn.pdf)).
The dead-module heuristic measured **~65% false positives** ecosystem-wide. Treat both as lead
lists, not verdicts. Full reasoning: [ecosystem rollup](../../../docs/audits/2026-08-22-ECOSYSTEM-ROLLUP.md).
