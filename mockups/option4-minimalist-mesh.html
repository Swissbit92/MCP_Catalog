Design Philosophy

This option follows Google Material Design's research-backed dark mode principles. The #121212 base color is specifically chosen to:

  • Avoid pure black (#000000) eye strain issues
  • Maintain 15.8:1 contrast ratio with white text (far exceeds WCAG AAA)
  • Maximize OLED battery savings (saves 40-60% vs light mode)
  • Provide a neutral canvas for content to shine

🎯 Best For: Users who prioritize performance, accessibility, and professionalism over visual excitement. Ideal for serious AI conversations and productivity use cases.
